## How we used the AI
We used Claude as a collaborator to suggest what to look at next from the initial setup, and to test our own readings of the plots against the data. We worked only on the existing model predictions, so the AI never ran or changed the model

## Speed versus effort
The AI produced working plots much faster than we could have written them but we had to check that they ran correctly and that they were relevant to our findings. The biggest struggle working with AI was due to misinterpretation of what we were asking it to do. Explaining what we actually wanted took significant time.

## Best practices
Bring our own reading first and ask the AI to test it. Ask for the smallest step and a list of defaults. Check AI facts with base rates, paired tests and sensitivity checks. Ask the AI to challenge the framing, not only to produce plots. 

## What we would do differently
Ask for a list of the AI's defaults at the start, add a calibration view, and audit a sample of misclassified utterances by hand to test why the always-wrong intents fail.
